About Janet

Janet's Planet was created to support women navigating change, stress and the search for deeper meaning. Through mindful movement, breathwork and immersive wellness experiences, Janet helps clients reconnect with themselves and create sustainable wellbeing.

With years of experience in yoga, meditation and holistic wellness practices, Janet offers a welcoming environment for beginners and experienced practitioners alike.
